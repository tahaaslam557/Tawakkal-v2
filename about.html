Who we are

A seafood brand built around freshness

TAWAKKAL is a fresh fish and seafood delivery brand. Our idea is simple: buying good fish should be easy, clean and convenient — without a trip to a crowded market.

We bring together everyday favourites like Rohu and Tilapia, premium choices like Surmai and Pomfret, and seafood such as prawns and crab, so you can plan family meals and special occasions in one place. Choose the weight you need, place your order online and we'll bring it to your door.

  • A focused selection of fish and seafood for home cooking
  • Clear per-KG pricing with flexible weight options
  • Simple online ordering with doorstep delivery
